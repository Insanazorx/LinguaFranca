# Literary formats, focused practice and sentence audio — 2026-10-23

Implemented six writing choices: story, novel excerpt, dialogue, theatre scene, essay and poem. The model receives genre-specific prompts; dialogue/play/poem also use native grammar sampling to preserve speaker labels, bounded turns and verse lines. The content itself is generated by Qwen, not assembled from canned passages. Poems permit shorter lengths than prose and retain line breaks.

Domain focus enables any combination of conjunction/preposition, noun/adjective/adverb, and verb/nonfinite groups. Every safe match in those groups is eligible without the balanced mode's density or consecutive-word caps. Positive language frequencies become relative weights; unavailable choices rebalance. The reading displays the actual count against eligible selected tokens. Existing Balanced settings, word-saving behavior and process-only reading sessions remain intact.

The word sheet now has Listen to sentence, English and Stop audio. Mixed speech queues English and target-language segments with corresponding locally available AVSpeechSynthesisVoice objects. Original speech reads only the source sentence. Poems use the selected line. Dismissal/backgrounding stops playback. Missing local voices produce a message; no voice download or cloud speech is initiated.

## Limits and next step

Focused means all safe available matches, not unconditional translation of every selected token. Adjectives currently require supported predicate forms; general attributive case/agreement is skipped. Present/infinitive and supported participial/converb forms work; English progressive phrases, unknown agreement, German separable/multiword syntax and general Russian aspect/past gender remain conservative. Coverage varies by passage; no forms or translations are invented to reach a percentage.

The small model may still produce awkward prose or miss topic/word-count targets. Format constraints govern structure, not semantics. Physical iPhone 13 performance, voice availability/quality and fresh-install airplane-mode audio have not been tested in this milestone. Next: try each focus combination and mixed English/German/Russian audio on the phone, collecting linguistic errors before expanding morphology. No new SRS implementation is included.
