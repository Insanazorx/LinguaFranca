# Aggressive and vocabulary writing — 2026-10-23

Aggressive replaces the former Domain focus name; persisted `focused` preferences decode as `aggressive`. It attempts every selected occurrence without frequency, density, adjacency or per-language count caps. Balanced frequencies, including zero, have no effect on Aggressive. Enabled languages participate equally, with reallocation when only one has a verified translation. International lookalikes remain excluded according to the earlier user preference. Actual coverage and the 100% attempt policy are displayed separately; uncertain translations are never forced.

The fourth combinable group, Preposition + object / verb, constructs an atomic reversible span. Supported noun complements include German definite/indefinite articles and attested noun case forms, and Russian attested noun case forms without English articles. Spatial `in`/`on` require a stative governor; `into` uses directional accusative. Other validated heads include with, without, through and spatial before. `without` plus a shared-subject nonfinite verb becomes German `ohne zu` + infinitive or Russian negative imperfective converb. Original offsets, popup saving and multilingual sentence audio work on the whole phrase. When this group is on, an unsupported preposition cannot be inserted alone. Modified/possessive/pronominal/quantified complements and unresolved government remain conservative.

Context fixes distinguish conditional `if` from indirect questions, and content-clause `that` from relative `that`. Before/after clause classification checks the immediate subject/verb pattern. The popup describes contrastive `but` rather than displaying the misleading source gloss “although”. These are deterministic checks, not a complete English dependency parser.

Vocabulary lists now open language groups. A group's top action generates original text entirely in that group's language using all its words. The existing local model handles this separate writing request; the lexical replacement engine is bypassed. Requests contain at most three vocabulary inputs and large lists produce multiple texts. Per the user's final correction, output has no minimum/maximum word count or length grammar: the model chooses the length. Native inference retains its bounded token/context resources; an unfinished trailing fragment is removed while required-expression coverage is still enforced. Native German/Russian prompts, fresh scenarios/inference contexts, session novelty checks, serialized generation, cancellation and a two-attempt limit are used. Displayed text must pass target-language detection, English-sentence rejection and whole-expression coverage. Attested dictionary inflections count; substrings and compound fragments do not. Completed texts remain only in AppEnvironment memory until process exit. Vocabulary and list persistence are unchanged.

## Limits and next step

The small bundled model produced awkward and grammatically incorrect phrases in some accepted German/Russian samples. Language/coverage validation cannot establish grammatical, semantic or CEFR quality. User review remains necessary before treating generated prose as a model answer. Exact safety checks may leave many selected English words unchanged; 100% attempt rate does not imply verified translation coverage of every token.

Phrase support is bounded: attributive adjective agreement, pronoun case, arbitrary verb government, separable verb syntax, general aspect and all spatial collocations are not implemented. Physical iPhone 13 performance, audio quality and fresh-install airplane-mode behavior were not tested in this milestone. Next step is the requested user test of Aggressive combinations, phrase popups and language-group writing in the installed simulator.
